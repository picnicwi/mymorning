Cybersecurity

97% breach rate, autonomous exploit generation, 91K+ attacks on LLM services — the AI threat surface has arrived.

Cybersecurity 01 / 04
Viking Cloud

97% of Organizations Report GenAI Security Breaches in 2026

According to Viking Cloud cybersecurity statistics, 97% of organizations reported GenAI security issues and breaches in 2026. GenAI data policy violation incidents more than doubled year-over-year, with average organizations now experiencing 223 incidents per month, reflecting the explosion of AI tool usage without adequate security controls.

Why it matters The near-universal breach rate signals that most organizations are deploying GenAI faster than they can secure it, creating systemic risk across industries including financial services.
Cybersecurity 02 / 04
CSA Research

LLMs Can Now Autonomously Discover and Exploit Vulnerabilities in Production Software

New Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) research documents that LLMs can now autonomously discover exploitable vulnerabilities in production software, generate working exploit code from vulnerability advisories, and execute multi-stage network attacks across realistic environments—outperforming most human red teamers in certain scenarios.

Why it matters The automation of exploit generation fundamentally changes the threat landscape, compressing the window between vulnerability disclosure and active exploitation and demanding AI-powered defenses.
Cybersecurity 03 / 04
GreyNoise

91,000+ Attack Sessions Targeting Exposed LLM Services (Oct 2025–Jan 2026)

GreyNoise research documented over 91,000 attack sessions targeting exposed LLM services in a three-month window from October 2025 to January 2026, demonstrating active reconnaissance and exploitation of AI infrastructure. RAG poisoning—injecting malicious documents into retrieval corpora—has emerged as a critical and underappreciated attack vector.

Why it matters As enterprises expose LLM APIs and RAG pipelines to the internet, they are creating new attack surfaces that traditional security tools are not designed to detect or block.
Cybersecurity 04 / 04
OWASP / Research

Jailbreak Success Rates Exceed 50% Across Major Models, Some Reaching 88%

Security research assessments revealed that certain jailbreak attack techniques achieve success rates exceeding 50% across major LLMs, with some reaching up to 88% in specific scenarios. OWASP's LLM Top 10 for 2026 highlights semantic attacks—where the meaning of a prompt rather than its format compromises systems—as a threat category traditional security controls cannot address.

Why it matters High jailbreak success rates mean content safety controls cannot be relied upon as a primary defense; enterprises must layer output validation, behavioral monitoring, and human oversight.
