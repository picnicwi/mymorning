Cybersecurity02 / 03
— OWASP / Cycode

Prompt Injection and RAG Poisoning Emerge as Top LLM Attack Vectors in 2026

OWASP's updated LLM Top 10 for 2026 highlights prompt injection and RAG poisoning as the most critical AI attack vectors, with some prompt injection techniques achieving success rates exceeding 88% across models. RAG poisoning has emerged as a critical new vector where adversaries inject malicious documents into retrieval corpora to manipulate AI outputs. Traditional syntactic security controls cannot address these semantic attacks, requiring new AI-native defense tooling.

Why it mattersOrganizations deploying RAG-based AI systems for financial advice, compliance, or customer service are exposed to knowledge base poisoning that can produce fraudulent outputs at scale.
