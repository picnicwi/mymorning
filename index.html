Cybersecurity

AI-powered attacks reach production scale — autonomous intrusions, self-propagating worms, and near-universal GenAI breaches.

Cybersecurity 01 / 03
Infosecurity Europe

AI Worm Compromises 73.8% of Simulated Enterprise Network Using Free Open-Weight LLM

At Infosecurity Europe 2026, Toronto researchers demonstrated a self-propagating AI worm powered by a free open-weight LLM that was able to compromise 73.8% of a simulated enterprise network autonomously. The worm used agentic reasoning to adapt its attack strategy in real time without human direction. Security experts called it the most alarming demonstration of AI-enabled autonomous attack capability to date.

Why It Matters Agentic attack tools that require no specialized expertise lower the bar for large-scale enterprise breaches, dramatically increasing the threat surface for financial institutions.
Cybersecurity 02 / 03
Security Research

97% of Organizations Reported GenAI Security Breaches in 2026

New research reveals 97% of organizations experienced GenAI-related security issues or breaches in 2026, with GreyNoise documenting over 91,000 attack sessions targeting exposed LLM services between October 2025 and January 2026. Key attack vectors include prompt injection, RAG corpus poisoning, and model theft through systematic output querying. OWASP has updated its LLM Top 10 list to reflect the rapidly evolving threat landscape.

Why It Matters Near-universal exposure to GenAI security incidents means financial institutions must treat LLM security as a core infrastructure risk, not an emerging concern.
Cybersecurity 03 / 03
Sysdig

First Confirmed Real-World LLM Agent Intrusion: Database Exfiltrated in Under One Hour

Sysdig's threat research team documented the first publicly confirmed real-world enterprise intrusion driven by an LLM agent, which moved from initial access through a vulnerable Python notebook to fully exfiltrating an internal database in under an hour across four network pivots, with no human operator directing individual steps. The attack demonstrated that agentic AI can execute sophisticated multi-stage intrusions autonomously. CVE-2026-27740, an LLM XSS vulnerability in Discourse AI, was also disclosed this week.

Why It Matters Fully autonomous AI-driven intrusions that complete in under an hour outpace most enterprise incident response capabilities, requiring AI-powered defensive automation to match.
